## Lesson sources
- Bismillah before eating, eat with the right hand: Sahih al-Bukhari 5376 / Sahih Muslim 2022 (https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5376)
- Alhamdulillah after eating and drinking: Sahih Muslim 2734 (https://sunnah.com/muslim:2734)
