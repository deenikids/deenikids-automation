Privacy Policy

App: Deeni & Friends Uploader (also called DEENIKIDS Uploader)
Last updated: 14 November 2026

What this app is

This app is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Deeni & Friends YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own children's videos to that channel using the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it uses

  • It signs in only to the channel owner's own Google account, with permission to upload and manage videos on that channel.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about any viewer, child or other person.
  • It does not read viewers' data, comments, contacts or watch history.
  • The access token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's own GitHub account and is used only to upload videos.

Children's privacy

All videos are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube, as YouTube requires. This app collects no information from children.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can remove this app's access at any time at Google security settings.
